# Backlog

Future ideas the owner has explicitly approved keeping for later.

A backlog entry is **not**:

- approved scope for any version;
- a roadmap commitment;
- permission to build it;
- evidence that it's technically feasible.

## Approval rule

**Only add an item after the owner explicitly approves that specific item for the backlog.** Brainstorming, agent suggestions, technical possibilities and ideas mentioned in passing don't qualify.

When the owner puts an item into a version, its done-means goes in the ledger and its approved rules into `DECISIONS.md`; then remove it here.

## Entry format

```
### AREA-001: <short name>
- **Status:** Deferred | Next up | Needs research
- **Approved:** <date>
<What it is, in a few sentences: the problem it solves and the direction the owner approved.>
Open questions: <what must be decided before it can be scoped>.
```
